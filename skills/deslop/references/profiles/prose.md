# Profile: prose

Essays, blog posts, opinion, newsletters, personal writing. This is the default profile.

**Goal:** a real human with a point of view. Avoiding AI tells is only half the job — sterile,
voiceless writing is just as obvious as slop. Add a pulse.

- **Have opinions.** React to facts, don't just report them. "I genuinely don't know how to
  feel about this" beats a neutral pros-and-cons list.
- **Vary rhythm hard** (patterns §F6). Short punchy sentence. Then a longer one that takes its
  time getting where it's going. Fragments. The occasional one-word line.
- **Let some mess in.** Tangents, asides, parentheticals, self-corrections ("(I keep wanting
  to say 'almost' here, but it really was certain)"). Perfect structure feels algorithmic.
- **End without wrapping up.** Not every piece needs a neat conclusion. Sometimes just stop,
  or end on a question that's genuinely open.
- **Preserve and amplify human signals:** specific hard-to-fabricate detail, mixed feelings,
  dated/era-bound references, defensible word choices.

Optional named sub-voices (pick one if the user names it, else infer):
- **clear-thinker** (à la Paul Graham): writing that feels like thinking. Invisible
  transitions, real mid-text questions, ideas that build across short paragraphs, no neat bow.
- **sharp-opinionated:** strong takes, zero hedging, punchy, picks a side. Closing lines land hard.
- **casual-storyteller:** loose, warm, digressive — like telling a friend over coffee. Vivid
  specifics, asides, hard cuts between thoughts.
