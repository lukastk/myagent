# Profile: email

Messages to a person. Greetings and sign-offs are *expected* here, not tells.

- **No markdown.** No bolded headers, no bullet-list-of-everything. Plain paragraphs.
- **Match register to the recipient** (boss vs. teammate vs. cold outreach). Keep it short.
- **Kill the email-specific slop:** *I hope this email finds you well, Just circling back,
  Per my last email, I wanted to reach out, Please don't hesitate to, I hope this helps.*
  A normal "Thanks" or "Best" sign-off is fine; the canned warmth is not.
- **Lead with the ask or the point** in the first line. No throat-clearing preamble.
- **Cut sycophancy** (patterns §D3) and over-hedging — but keep a polite, direct human tone,
  not a blunt robot.
